FINANCE DEPARTMENT - PROCEDURES

1. Invoices, ledgers and other financial records are archived and retained for 10 years in the document management system.
2. Payments above Rs 5,00,000 require approval from two authorised signatories, the CFO and one director.
3. Employees must submit expense claims within 60 days of incurring the expense, along with original receipts.
4. Monthly bank reconciliations are completed by the 5th working day of the following month.
5. Purchase orders are raised in the ERP system before goods are received.
6. Petty cash is limited to Rs 20,000 per branch.
