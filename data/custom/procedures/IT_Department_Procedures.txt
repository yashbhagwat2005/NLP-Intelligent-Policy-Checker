IT DEPARTMENT - STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (v3.2)

1. Account creation. New user accounts are created by the IT helpdesk after a ticket is raised by the reporting manager.
2. Passwords. Every user password must have a minimum of 8 characters and include one number.
3. Passwords are automatically set to expire and must be changed every 60 days.
4. Remote access. Staff working from home connect through the company VPN. Multi-factor authentication is optional for VPN users and may be enabled on request.
5. Incident handling. Any suspected security incident, such as malware or phishing, must be reported to the security team within 4 hours of detection.
6. Backups. Backups of all servers are taken every night and stored at the secondary data centre.
7. Laptops are re-imaged before being assigned to a new employee.
8. Offboarding. When an employee leaves, all their system accounts and access rights are disabled on their last working day by the IT team.
9. Access reviews. Department heads perform quarterly access reviews of user permissions in all business applications.
10. Software installation requires approval from the IT manager.
