HUMAN RESOURCES - PROCESS MANUAL

1. Onboarding. New joiners submit identity documents, bank details and emergency contacts on the HR portal.
2. Employee records, including salary and medical information, are stored in an encrypted database using AES-256 encryption at rest.
3. HR may share employee contact details with insurance partners to send them marketing offers.
4. Leave requests are approved by the reporting manager through the HR portal.
5. Performance appraisals are conducted once a year in April.
6. Exit interviews are conducted by the HR business partner for every departing employee.
7. Grievances can be raised confidentially through the ethics helpline.
