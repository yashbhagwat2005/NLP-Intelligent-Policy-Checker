WAREHOUSE OPERATIONS - PROCEDURES

1. Goods received are checked against the purchase order and entered in the inventory system the same day.
2. The warehouse team uses a common login, store01, on the inventory terminal so that any shift member can update stock.
3. Stock counts are performed every month and differences above 2 percent are escalated to the operations manager.
4. Forklifts may only be operated by certified drivers.
5. Damaged goods are moved to the quarantine area and recorded in the damage log.
