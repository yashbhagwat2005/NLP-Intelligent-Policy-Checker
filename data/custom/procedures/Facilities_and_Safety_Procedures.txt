FACILITIES, SECURITY AND SAFETY - PROCEDURES

1. CCTV. Footage from CCTV cameras at all entrances is stored on the local recorder for 90 days and then overwritten.
2. Visitors must sign the visitor register and wear a visitor badge at all times.
3. Fire evacuation drills are held once a year for all floors.
4. Every workplace injury or near miss is recorded in the incident register on the same day by the floor safety officer.
5. Wearing safety helmets and gloves in the warehouse is encouraged but not mandatory.
6. Fire extinguishers are inspected every month by an external vendor.
7. First aid kits are available at every reception desk.
